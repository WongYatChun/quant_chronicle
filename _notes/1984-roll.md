## Why?

Can transaction prices reveal a spread when quotes are unavailable?

## Who?

Roll, A Simple Implicit Measure of the Effective Bid-Ask Spread in an Efficient Market (1984).

## How?

In the simple model $P_t=M_t+c\epsilon_t$, the efficient midpoint has independent innovations; trade signs are independent symmetric draws, independent of those innovations. Then:

$$\operatorname{Cov}(\Delta P_t,\Delta P_{t-1})=-c^2,\qquad \widehat s=2\sqrt{-\widehat{\operatorname{Cov}}(\Delta P_t,\Delta P_{t-1})}.$$

## So what?

Bid–ask bounce creates negative return autocovariance. Positive sample covariance produces no real-valued estimate under this formula. Persistent signs, changing spreads, and informative trades can invalidate its interpretation.
