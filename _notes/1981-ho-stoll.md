## Why?

How should a dealer quote while holding risky inventory?

## Who?

Ho and Stoll, Optimal Dealer Pricing under Transactions and Return Uncertainty (1981).

## How?

The original model optimizes terminal utility with stochastic transactions. A simplified mean–variance illustration over a fixed holding horizon $\tau$ is:

$$J(q)=qm-\frac{\gamma}{2}q^2\sigma^2\tau,\qquad \partial_qJ=m-\gamma q\sigma^2\tau.$$

## So what?

A long dealer lowers their valuation to encourage inventory reduction. This illustration is not the paper’s exact dynamic solution; the original model also incorporates uncertain transaction timing.
