## Why?

Why does immediate execution have a price?

## Who?

Harold Demsetz, The Cost of Transacting (1968).

## How?

A liquidity supplier bridges the timing gap between buyers and sellers. With ask $A$, bid $B$, and midpoint $m=(A+B)/2$:

$$s=A-B,\qquad A-m=m-B=\frac{s}{2}.$$

## So what?

The half-spread measures the cost of crossing from the midpoint under symmetric quotes. It is not the dealer’s net profit: inventory risk, adverse selection, and other costs remain.
