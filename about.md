# A notebook for connecting ideas

This chronicle follows four questions: what determines returns, how to allocate capital, how to price contingent claims, and how trading shapes markets.

Each entry uses **Why → Who → How → So what**, followed by original references. The mathematics prioritizes the central mechanism; simplified formulations are labeled explicitly.

These developments overlap. A newer model does not automatically replace an older one. Assumptions and connections matter as much as dates.

This first edition contains three opening notes. Further entries will be added incrementally.
